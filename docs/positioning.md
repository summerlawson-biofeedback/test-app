POSITIONING
Oura tells you you're stressed. Calm helps if you remember to open it.
PulsePause notices, steps in, and shows you how fast you recovered,
using the device you already own.

WHAT WE BORROW
- From Calm: atmosphere as the brand. People should open PulsePause for how it
  feels (inner-weather sky, breathing visuals, gentle haptics), not just for data.
  Future path: employer/team wellness (executives, athletes, corporate groups).
- From Oura: resilience framing (how fast you bounce back, not just how stressed
  you are) and a broad, neutral audience in a performance register.
- From Gentler Streak / How We Feel: compassionate tone, color-coded check-ins,
  privacy stated upfront.

WHAT WE OWN (our differentiators; protect these in every build decision)
1. No new hardware required. Works with Apple Watch / Apple Health data people
   already have, and the "I'm activated" button works with no wearable at all.
2. In-the-moment intervention is the core product, not an add-on: detect ->
   offer strategy -> recheck after ~5 min -> reinforce recovery.
3. Fuel is linked to stress alerts: if last fuel was 4+ hours ago, suggest fuel
   first, then a breathing strategy.
